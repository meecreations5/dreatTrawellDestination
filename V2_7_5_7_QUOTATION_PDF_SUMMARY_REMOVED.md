# Dream Trawell CRM V2.7.5.7 - Quotation PDF Summary Removed

## Change
Removed the compact travel/commercial summary block from the customer quotation PDF only.

The PDF no longer shows the separate cards/box for:
- Destination
- Travel Month
- Check In
- Check Out
- Nights
- Pax
- Quotation Amount

## Unchanged
- Quotation form and saved quotation data remain unchanged.
- Prepared For section remains.
- Customer quotation/itinerary body remains.
- Dream Trawell branding and signature remain.
- Internal vendor cost, GP, margin and internal notes remain excluded.
- Generate PDF / Print workflow remains unchanged.
- Role and Team/User access behavior remains unchanged.
- Login pages remain unchanged.
