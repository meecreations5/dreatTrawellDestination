# V2.7.5.12.3 - Role-Based Workstreams & Responsibility Tweak

Baseline: V2.7.5.12.2 Daily Activity & Follow-ups.

## Why this tweak
DreamTrawell has two distinct execution workstreams and they should not be measured with the same KPIs:

1. Engagement / Relationship Executive
   - Build rapport with Travel Agents.
   - Increase touchbase and meaningful connections.
   - Discover requirements.
   - Create qualified Leads.

2. Lead & Operations Executive
   - Manage assigned Leads.
   - Quote / Re-Quote / Lead follow-up.
   - Vendor coordination.
   - Conversion and on-ground operations.

## Existing data model retained
No parallel handover model was added.

- Existing Lead owner/creator = Lead Generated By (permanent credit).
- Existing Lead assignee = Lead Managed By (current responsibility).
- Reassignment continues to change only the assignee.
- Existing lead ownership, quotation, payment, SPOC sync and timeline logic remain intact.

## Key changes

### Role labels
- `employee` is presented as Engagement / Relationship Executive.
- `operation_executive` is presented as Lead & Operations Executive.
- Existing compatibility storage using `role: employee` + `accessRole: operation_executive` is respected by the shared team directory.

### Action Center
- Engagement users default to Engagement follow-ups.
- Lead & Operations users see the full Lead + On-ground execution queue.
- Once a Lead has a current assignee, operational task responsibility follows that assignee.
- The Lead creator no longer keeps seeing assignee-owned operational next actions merely because they are the permanent owner.
- Legacy/unassigned work still falls back to the creator so old records are not orphaned.

### Team Dashboard
Engagement workstream KPIs:
- Engagements
- Calls Connected
- Unique Agents Touched
- Meaningful Engagements
- Requirements Generated
- Qualified Leads Created

Lead & Operations workstream KPIs:
- Open Assigned Leads
- New Leads Received
- Quote Work
- Re-Quote Work
- Lead Follow-ups
- Operations Work Completed

Lead queues use current responsibility rather than permanent creator ownership after handover.

### Productivity Pulse
Engagement pulse focuses on relationship/output metrics and Leads generated.
Lead & Operations pulse focuses on Work Actions, Completed Work, Quote, Re-Quote, Lead Follow-up and On-ground work.
Next-focus options are also workstream specific.

### Manager reporting
- Daily Action Center shows Workstream and Primary Output per team member.
- Productivity Pulse summary is workstream aware.
- Admin Team Performance no longer applies the same score logic to every role.
- Engagement score emphasizes connection, meaningful engagement, requirements and Leads generated.
- Lead & Operations score emphasizes managed Leads, managed quotations, wins and GP contribution.
- Manager table separately exposes Leads Generated vs Leads Managed.

### Lead UI terminology
- Lead Owner is surfaced as Lead Generated By.
- Current Assignee is surfaced as Lead Managed By.

## Compatibility
No Firestore migration is required for this tweak.
Existing fields are reused and existing CRM modules are preserved.
